The Fightlytics glove mark: an orange (`--accent`) outlined glove. `size` is the pixel square (default 34; strokes thicken below 36px). `mono` draws it in `currentColor` with no fill, for use on a coloured or single-ink surface. Use it alone as an app icon or avatar; next to the wordmark use `LogoLockup` instead.
