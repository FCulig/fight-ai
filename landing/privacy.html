Privacy.

Fightlytics is a free-time project, not a company. This page explains what it collects, why, where it’s kept, and how to get it deleted. The short version: only what’s needed to run the waitlist and the app. No ads, no selling data, and your fight videos are never used for training unless you say yes.

01

What we collect.

  • Waitlist: email address, signup date, and your monthly-updates choice.
  • Account: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. Google handles your password; we never see it.
  • Your content: fight videos you upload, and the analyses, labels and notes created from them.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser details in server logs, kept briefly for security.
  • Usage statistics: anonymous page views via Umami. No cookies, and no way to tell who you are.
02

Why, and legal basis.

Under the GDPR, every use of your data needs a legal basis. Here’s ours:

PurposeLegal basis
Waitlist and invite emailsSteps you asked us to take before using the service (Art. 6(1)(b))
Running your account and analysing your videosProviding the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b))
Monthly product updatesYour consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Unsubscribe from any email.
Improving models with your videosYour consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Off unless you turn it on.
Security logs and anonymous statisticsLegitimate interest in keeping the site safe and working (Art. 6(1)(f))
03

Fight videos.

Videos and analyses are private to your account. There’s no sharing or public profile.

We use them only to produce your analysis. If you turn on “Help improve Fightlytics”, your videos and labels may also be used to train our detection models. You can turn it off any time; future training then excludes your data.

Videos often show other people. Only upload footage you have the right to use. If you appear in a video someone else uploaded and want it removed, contact us.

04

Who handles it.

We don’t sell or share your data. These services process it on our behalf:

ServiceWhat it does
Oracle CloudHosts the app, database, videos and our own email server. EU region.
GoogleSign-in. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
Umami CloudAnonymous, cookieless page statistics.

Emails are sent from our own mail server, not a third-party newsletter tool.

05

How long we keep it.

  • Waitlist email: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Account data: until you close your account.
  • Videos and analyses: until you delete them, or close your account.
  • Server logs: a short period for security, then deleted.

If the project shuts down, everything is deleted.

06

Your rights.

You can ask to see, correct, delete or download your data, to restrict or object to how it’s used, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the data protection authority in your EU country.

07

Cookies.

We set one essential cookie to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies, so there’s no cookie banner.

08

Age.

There’s no minimum age to use Fightlytics. If you’re under 16 (or your country’s age of digital consent), ask a parent or guardian before signing up or uploading videos.

09

Changes.

If this page changes in a way that matters, we’ll update the date at the top and email account holders before it takes effect.
