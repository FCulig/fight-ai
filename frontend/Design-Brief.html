Design brief · App redesign

Bring the app up to the new brand.

The marketing site now has a clear visual language: black ground, one ember accent, heavy Manrope display type, huge numbers and motion driven by scrolling. This brief sets out how to carry that language into the product: Studio, the analysis player, reports, and later Broadcast.

01Goals

  • One product, one voice. Going from the site to the app should feel seamless. The same palette, type and components continue from the marketing pages into the app.
  • Data before decoration. The app is a working tool for coaches and analysts. Numbers, events and footage are the heroes, and the interface around them stays quiet.
  • Instant, legible answers. Any fight stat should be readable in under a second, from across a gym.
  • Nothing that reads as generic "AI". No gradient text, glow halos, drifting background orbs, glassmorphism on every surface or rainbow palettes.

05Logo & brand mark

  • Use LogoLockup (horizontal) in the app nav at 26px, and LogoStack on sign-in and empty states.
  • GloveGlyph (single ink, inherits the text color) is punctuation for eyebrows, section labels and the "processing" state. Use no more than one per view region.
  • GloveWatermark is an outline at 4–5% opacity, for large empty states only. It never sits behind data.
  • The wordmark stays solid ink. No gradient fills, and don't recolor the glove outside the ember/ink options.

06Shape, surface & components

Surfaces

  • Opaque panels on black. Blur only on the sticky nav (blur(18px) saturate(1.6), 72% black).
  • 1px --x-line border; active state raises to --x-panel-2 with an 18% white border.
  • Radius: cards 28px, inner vis wells 18px, inputs and buttons pill (999px), chips 999px, video 12–16px.
  • Shadow only on floating media: 0 40px 120px rgba(0,0,0,.8).

Buttons & controls

  • Primary: ember pill, 44px tall (30px compact), 700 weight. One per view.
  • Secondary: ghost pill, 28% white outline.
  • Text link: ember, with an arrow that nudges 3px on hover.
  • Press state: scale 0.97. No glow on hover, just a lighter fill.
  • Hit targets at least 44px on touch.

Stat tiles

  • A huge number, a unit in ember, then a label, then a one-line note. Nothing else.
  • Left-aligned. Numbers tick in when they appear (ease-out cubic, around 600ms).
  • Fighter comparison: value · label · value, with a single split bar under it (ember vs blue).

Data views

  • Timeline: round-divided track, tick per event, grappling as a soft band, white playhead.
  • Event feed: newest at bottom, row = type · frame · sentence with fighter bolded.
  • Tracking boxes: 1.5px corner color, tag + confidence in mono.

07Motion

The site tells its story through scrolling; the app should respond quickly. Keep the same easing and restraint, but tie motion to user actions rather than scroll position.

Word revealStatement paragraphNot used in the app
  • Easing: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Durations: 150ms (hover), 300ms (state), 600ms (entrance).
  • Only animate transform and opacity. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Video and data never wait on an animation to finish.

08Voice

  • Short, declarative, fight-literate: "Every strike. Counted." / "The whole fight, broken down."
  • Name the move, fighter and round. Write "Blue landed 3 low kicks in R2", not "Insights detected".
  • Periods give headlines their punch. No exclamation marks, and no "AI-powered", "revolutionary" or "unlock".
  • Button labels are verbs: "Upload a fight", "Try Studio", "Export report".

09Do / Don't

Do

  • Black ground, one ember accent
  • Large solid numerals
  • Hairline borders, big radii
  • Generous negative space around a single focal element
  • Real footage and real data in mockups

Don't

  • Gradient text or gradient buttons
  • Glow shadows and neon bloom
  • Blurred orbs, film grain, glass on every card
  • Cyan and purple, or other off-brand accents
  • Icon grids padded with filler stats

10Deliverables

  • Hi-fi screens for all P0 areas at 1440 and 390 widths.
  • Clickable prototype: upload → processing → player → report.
  • Component sheet (buttons, inputs, stat tile, event row, timeline, tracking box, nav) with every state.
  • A token file that extends app/v2.css. No new colors without sign-off.

Open questions: the production app URL (the site currently links to a placeholder), whether a light theme is needed for printed reports, and the Broadcast launch timing.
